Designed for reuse, with honest limits.

FitRelay is a free prototype for people and community exchanges sharing pre-owned clothing. A simple measurement comparison may help shortlist items before a pickup. It has not been tested for reducing textile waste, travel, or returns. Fabric, cut, condition, and personal preference still matter.

How to measure

  1. Choose a garment that already fits you comfortably.
  2. Lay it flat and measure chest width, shoulder-to-hem length, and shoulder-to-cuff sleeve.
  3. Compare like with like, then inspect the garment in person when possible.

Context: EPA textile waste data. The EPA has not evaluated FitRelay.
